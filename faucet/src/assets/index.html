Qnero testnet faucet

Testnet funds

This sends QNR to one qn1 address, once per address per .

Every payment on Qnero is private, and a private payment is a zero-knowledge proof. The faucet proves one at a time: about ten seconds to prove, and then up to one block, which is two minutes, to settle. The page waits for both.

What it does not do

  • It pays one address at a time and refuses a second claim inside the cooldown.
  • It cannot pay transparent QNR. There is no transparent transfer on this chain, so the funds appear in your wallet once it has synced, and there is no public ledger entry to look up.
  • These tokens are worth nothing. The network may be reset without notice, which takes every balance on it with it.
