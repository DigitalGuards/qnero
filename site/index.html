Qnero

Monero's principles, rebuilt without elliptic curves.

Every transfer is shielded. Sender, recipient and amount are hidden. There is no transparent option for users, and no elliptic curve anywhere in the transaction path. Emission is proof of work, with no stake, no validators and no foundation keys in consensus.

01 / What it is

Four priorities, in this order

  1. Private by default, every spend a proof. A call that moves transparent value between accounts a user chooses is refused by the runtime. The one transparent payout that survives the filter is the genesis vesting claim, whose payee and amount are both fixed at genesis. A tech referendum dispatches as Root and the filter does not reach Root.
  2. Proof of work, with no stake and no validators. RandomX, stock rx/0, the algorithm every CPU miner already runs. No foundation keys in consensus, and no algorithm nobody has hardware or software for.
  3. Post-quantum from genesis. Hash-based proofs, lattice signatures and lattice encapsulation. There is nothing in the transaction path for Shor to break.
  4. Audited parts only: standardized primitives and firm-audited circuits. Those audited parts are upstream's. The audits are Eiger on the Wormhole circuits (2026-03-20) and a Substrate audit of the chain (2026-05-13). No external audit of the Qnero delta exists, and that delta is the leaf circuit's note fragments, the public-input layouts at all three layers, the aggregator rules and pallet-shielded. Plonky2's 100-bit security here is a conjecture.
02 / For Monero users

The mental model carries over almost intact

Ten pieces of Monero and what each becomes here. The last two are the habits that do not carry.

Monero to Qnero, piece by piece
Monero Qnero
Spend key and view key Still split. Spending authority is ask = H("qnero/ask", sk) and the address half that receives is pk = H(PK, ak, nk). The viewing side is an ML-KEM decapsulation key opening the AEAD ciphertext on each output, with a separate nk for spent status. Scanning is the same linear trial decryption of every output.
Ring size 16 with decoys Gone. A spend proves Merkle membership in a 4-ary Poseidon tree whose leaves are every note ever appended, so the set it hides in is the pool itself.
Key image A nullifier, nf = H(NF, nk, rho, r), published into UsedNullifiers. Same role: the chain refuses one it has already seen, so a note spends once.
RingCT with Bulletproofs+ One proof. A note commits as cm = H(CM, H(NOTE, pk, rho, r), v), balance is proved in circuit, and each output range-checks to 62 bits inside the spend proof.
Stealth address over ECDH An ML-KEM encapsulation per output and an AEAD note ciphertext.
Coinbase to the miner Still a note. One per block, minted from a required inherent, its value and its block public, the recipient hidden.
RandomX proof of work RandomX, stock rx/0, the same algorithm and the same constants. A rig that mines Monero mines Qnero with a config change.
120-second blocks 120 seconds, decided 2026-09-14. The RandomX seed epoch of 2048 blocks is Monero's 2.84 days to the hour.
Unlock time Absent. An anchor expires after BlockHashWindow, 256 blocks, which is 8.5 hours at a 120-second target, and that is the only waiting rule.
Tail emission Undesigned. The emission schedule inherited from the upstream chain runs against MAX_SUPPLY, so the question Monero answered with a tail is open here.

Supply is capped at 21M QNR on the upstream decay schedule, and emission at genesis supply is 411 quanta a block against the 120-second target. Every unit of value created after genesis is a note, minted one per block to whoever mined it. The genesis allocation is undecided: a 2 percent placeholder sits in the design and may be removed. Tail emission is undecided as well. Both are open questions, and this site promises neither answer.

03 / Why

Why the cryptography was replaced

Monero's privacy is Ed25519 in four places
  • CLSAG ring signatures with key images
  • RingCT Pedersen commitments with Bulletproofs+
  • ECDH stealth addresses
  • The in-progress FCMP++ curve trees

None of the four has a production-grade post-quantum replacement. Lattice RingCT designs, MatRiCT+ and Raptor among them, remain unaudited papers with transactions in the tens of KB.

Hash-based FRI proving is post-quantum by construction and has production implementations, and a shielded pool in Zcash Orchard semantics needs only a hash function inside the circuit. Qnero keeps the policy and rebuilds the machinery from hashes and lattices.

The base is an upstream post-quantum Substrate stack: ML-DSA-87 accounts, post-quantum Noise over ML-KEM for p2p, a 4-ary Poseidon commitment tree and a nullifier set. Field Goldilocks. Hash Poseidon at Quantus parameters, which an audit firm has reviewed. Proofs Plonky2 over FRI, at a conjectured 100 bits. Every primitive is hash-based or lattice-based.

04 / Mechanism

One leaf proof is one shielded transfer

Up to 2 input notes, exactly 2 outputs, a public fee, and 26 public inputs fixed in the circuit layout. Per real input the circuit derives the address half from the spend key, recomputes the commitment, walks a 16-level Merkle path to the anchored header's tree root, and emits the nullifier. Outputs range-check to 62 bits, so the balance equation cannot wrap the field. Two recursive layers sit above: N leaf proofs make a private batch, the on-chain transaction unit, and n of those make a public batch.

Leaf circuit 320 gates 105500-byte proof, 2.2 ms to verify
Private batch, N = 6 150908 bytes 3.34 to 3.61 s to prove on four threads

05 / Disclosure

What a block publishes

Private by default is a policy with edges, and the edges are public. A reader deciding what Qnero is should be able to find them in one place.

  • A shield names its payer, its value and the leaf it created. The entry is the sharp edge: value that enters the pool that way is linked to the account it came from at the moment it enters.
  • A coinbase note publishes its value and its block. The recipient sits inside an inner the chain cannot open, and no event or storage item names a block's author.
  • Every settled nullifier is public. The set records that some note was spent. It does not say which one.
  • A settlement publishes each slot's two nullifiers, two commitments, both leaf indices and both ciphertexts. A payment and its change are therefore publicly a pair. Which of the two is which stays hidden, because the wallet draws the payment's output slot per spend.
  • A refused transparent transfer still enters a block. The call filter is checked at dispatch, so the extrinsic pays its fee and then fails, and its arguments stay in the body forever. One mistaken attempt publishes exactly the sender, recipient and amount triple the policy exists to deny.
